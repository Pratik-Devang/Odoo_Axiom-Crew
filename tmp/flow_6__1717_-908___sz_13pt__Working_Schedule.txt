- An employee can have multiple contracts over time.
- Payroll should use the contract applicable to the selected payroll period.

Working Schedule
- Working Schedules need List and Form views. Clicking a row should open that schedule.
- List should surface name, calendar type, days/week, hours/week, company and status.
- Form defines the weekly pattern: day, start/end time, optional break and hours. Weekly
hours should be derived from the schedule.
- A schedule can be assigned to an Employee/Contract and used by Attendance and Payroll.
Shift, flexible-time and other rules are open to the participant.
